Stability release: a full audit of the app found and fixed 29 behavior bugs, including several critical ones.

- Fixed camera/microphone access not working on websites (video calls, voice input, etc.)
- Fixed file upload fields not working on websites
- Fixed the back button sometimes showing a blank white screen
- Fixed background tabs occasionally affecting the address bar, progress bar, or showing the wrong error/security warning on the active tab
- Fixed the Home button not always navigating home
- Fixed gamepad/TV remote input being unresponsive right after launch
- Fixed several download manager and tab-switching glitches and crashes

No new features in this release — just making everything that already exists work correctly.
